# Echelon (EVE Pilot Console)

A wing console for **Fiofka** and **Decxus**, two pilots who fly EVE Online together: GitHub Pages frontend, Cloudflare Worker backend.

Live: https://matheusfiorin.github.io/eve-journal/

Local setup and checks on Windows: [development environment](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md).

## What it shows

The first visit asks **who's flying** (Fiofka or Decxus) in a full-screen picker. The browser remembers the choice, every view centres on that pilot, and the wingmate's plans sit right alongside. Switch from the top bar.

Seven destinations sit in the left rail (a bottom bar on phones): **Bridge**, **Wing** (Crew map, Goals), **Pilot** (Training, Skills, Clones), **Earn** (Build, Mine, Explore, Trade, Jobs), **Navigate** (Star map, Haul plan), **Ledger** and **Logbook**. Each view has its own address (`#earn/mine`), and old links like `#mining` still work. **Ctrl/Cmd K** searches screens, actions and systems; `g` then a letter jumps to a destination, `1`–`5` switches views, and `?` lists every shortcut.

- **Bridge** — your title (rank + specialty from your skill points), training alert, ship and position, your **current fit** (high, mid, low, rig and subsystem slots, drones, fighters and holds, read from the ship's own assets, with **Copy as EFT**), and your wing: the wingmate's training, wing net worth and combined skill points. A wingmate who shares at consent level 2 also shares their ship, and at level 3 their fit.
- **Wing → Crew map** — wing net worth, holdings by category, what we own (grouped, with each owner's portrait) and each pilot's role breakdown.
- **Earn → Trade** — opportunities from your pilot's current system (or a trade hub): haul & flip routes ranked by profit per jump, station trading spreads, and manufacturing margins, all sized to your budget (your wallet unless you type another). Fees come from your Accounting and Broker Relations; cargo defaults to your current ship. The **broker fee by hub** panel shows the effective fee per trade hub: 3% − 0.3% × Broker Relations − 0.03% × faction standing − 0.02% × corporation standing toward the station's owner, never under 1% (formula from EVE University's Trading page, unmodified standings). Station owners come from ESI (`/universe/stations/{id}/`, `/corporations/{id}/` for the faction) and are cached in the browser since they never change; when an owner or the standings are unknown the fee is skills only and says 'standings not applied'. Spreads and builds use the fee of the station they trade in.
- **Earn → Planets** (`#earn/planets`) — every colony of each pilot (the wingmate's only at consent level 3): planet type, upgrade level, pins, extractors with expiry (expired flagged, under 24 hours warned), output per hour where the pins give it, and storage fill. ESI only refreshes a colony when it is viewed in game, so each card says 'Last updated in game X ago'. A Discord alert fires once when an extractor runs out (planet type only, no place names).
- **Earn → Sell** (`#earn/sell`) — where to sell what your ships carry and the hangar you pick up from, and each ship's route, for the most ISK after sales tax and expected gank loss. How to use it: tick the ships (one per connected pilot, plus the wingmate at consent level 3) and set cargo m³; the items come from each hold and from the hangar at the docked station. Press Change to pick up from another station (hangar contents for it arrive with the next sync, up to 10 minutes), or Paste a list (`Name<TAB>qty` or `5 x Name`) for anything not shown. Set ISK per jump (what one jump is worth to you; default 1M), the most jumps and low-sec, then read the plan cards: Sell here, Best, Most ISK and points between. Pick a card to see each ship's stops, what to pick up and sell where (including buy orders in another station that the ship can fill from where it docks), what stays unsold and why. Open in Star map adds the routes as layers; Send to autopilot uses that pilot's own session. Buy orders are public ESI books per region, cached for 5 minutes, at most 150 requests per plan.
- **Earn → Build** — an early-game plan for the chosen pilot: alpha/omega detection, job slots, fees, the cheapest place to build, a stage plan by wallet, the next skills to train, and the best NPC-sold T1 blueprints to start with. Picks are sized to the budget (your wallet by default): a small wallet buys fewer runs, and picks it can't cover at all are hidden unless you ask for them. A wing build plan fills each pilot's build slots within their wallet, skipping blueprints they lack the skills for and never giving both pilots the same product. Each pick shows its research plan (ME target, time, cost), required skills, the full material bill (bought in one region), margin, profit per day and payback. Sale prices are capped at 15% over the 7-day average, and thin, spiking, crowded or low-margin markets are flagged and ranked lower. It lists the cheapest safe NPC factories and labs within 10 high-sec jumps, and every pick has a trip plan: where to buy the blueprint, research, buy materials, build and sell, each leg with its jump-by-jump route (coloured by security) and a Dotlan map link. Blueprints you already own count: a pick you hold shows `Owned (ME x/TE y)` or `Copy, N runs`, skips the buy-the-blueprint leg and its cost, and its margin, payback and research use your own ME and TE (a copy is not researched and caps the batch at its runs). The wing build plan gives a product to the pilot who owns its blueprint first, and **My blueprints** lists everything each pilot holds (filter box searches it). A wingmate's blueprints show only at consent level 2, like the fit.
- **Earn → Mine** — the best belts within reach of each pilot right now: which ores can spawn there (by security and empire), ISK per hour for your ship and skills, ore valued at the best of selling raw, compressed or reprocessed, last hour's kills and traffic per system, a jump-by-jump route, a ship comparison with what to train next, and the last 30 days of your mining ledger valued at today's prices. High-sec by default; tick **Include low-sec** to widen it.
- **Earn → Explore** — where to scan for relic and data sites. ESI has no signature counts, so “least explored” means least travelled: systems are ranked by jumps over ~24 hours (decayed hourly history), ship kills (weighted by a careful/normal/bold setting), distance and dead ends, and systems you or your wingmate visited recently (the backend keeps a 7-day location trail; **Mark scanned** adds your own) sink. A scanning loop visits the best picks in the fewest jumps and returns to your start, with time, risk, 3D view, Dotlan and **Send to autopilot**. It suggests your race's explorer frigate and the exploration skills to train next. High-sec is the default; tick low-sec or null-sec when you're ready, and live Thera/Turnur wormhole connections from EVE-Scout show the way into wormhole space.
- **Navigate → Haul plan** — plan a trip through any number of stops (optionally in the best order, or back to the start) and compare three routes: safest, balanced and shortest. Each system's risk combines the backend's 24-hour and 7-day kill history (ESI, sampled hourly) per passing ship, how attractive your cargo is to gankers, your hull, and live gate camps: zKillboard's last hour of kills in every region on the route, counted per gate you actually use, flagged for smartbombs and interdictors. Routes minimise jumps × the value of your time + cargo value × −ln(chance of arriving). Every system links to zKillboard, the trip opens on Dotlan, and **Send to autopilot** sets the route in game.
- **Navigate → Star map** — a Dotlan-style map of New Eden built from CCP's own 2D region layout and 3D star positions. Region maps show every system with a glowing security light, gates, constellation borders and links to neighbouring regions; drag (one finger) to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and **Fit** shows the whole region. **Region in 3D** and **New Eden in 3D** orbit the real star positions (drag to turn, pinch or scroll to zoom, two fingers to pan) with auto-rotation that stops when you prefer reduced motion; the 3D map only redraws while something moves. **List** shows the region as a sortable table (security, kills over 1 h/24 h/7 days, jumps, belts, NPC stations and services, build index, incursions and faction warfare). Overlays from live ESI: ship, pod and NPC kills and jumps in the last hour, ~24-hour ship kills, sovereignty (coloured per owner), faction warfare, incursions, the manufacturing index, belts and NPC stations; the legend names the security bands and each overlay's scale, and says when a live feed is unavailable or an overlay is empty in the region. Jump range (Black Ops, jump freighter, capitals, supers) highlights low/null-sec systems in light-years. The address keeps the region, system, overlay, jump range and view (`#navigate/map?r=10000002&s=30000142&o=shipKills&v=3d`), so links can be shared and **Back** steps through your selections; the view, overlay and jump range you last used are remembered in this browser. Routes opened with **Open in Star map** stack as coloured layers (up to four) with A/B badges, hop numbers and a note where the route leaves the region; the bar on the map fits each route, sends it to the autopilot or removes it. Click a system for its panel: security, constellation, live stats, sovereignty, faction warfare, incursion, industry indices, gates, NPC stations and their services, a route from your location, **Haul here**, **Haul from**, **Add as stop**, **Set destination**, Dotlan and zKillboard. On phones the map fills the screen: search and **Layers** float on top, zoom, Fit and Locate me sit in a thumb-sized stack, and the system panel is a bottom sheet you drag between peek, half and full. Keyboard: Tab reaches the map once, arrow keys follow gates (and region exits) and Enter selects; in 3D the arrows turn, +/− zoom and Enter picks the star in the middle. Search (**Ctrl/Cmd K**) shows a system on the Star map; **Shift+Enter** opens it in 3D.
- **Routes everywhere** — every route in Market, Industry, Mining and Hauling previews as a sketch when you hover it, and **3D view** opens it in a 3D modal with its jump strip; system names open their panel; **Open in Star map** carries the route onto the full map.
- **Pilot → Training** — the active skill with progress, a queue timeline, and alerts when the queue is empty, paused, or runs out within 24 hours.
- **Pilot → Skills** — the full skill sheet grouped by skill group, with levels, SP per skill and group, and search.
- **Ledger** — balance, the latest 50 journal entries and market transactions, your **contracts** (outstanding, in progress and recently finished or expired; courier collateral and reward; anything expiring within 24 hours is flagged; a contract's items load only when you ask), a summary of your market orders, and current industry jobs.
- **Earn → Trade → My orders** — your open market orders (item, buy or sell, price, volume left, place, days left) with an **outbid** check against the last market scan (“Outbid by 1.00 ISK”, “Best price” or “Not checked” when that region isn't scanned), and the last 30 days of filled, expired and cancelled orders.
- **Pilot → Clones** — attributes and remaps, active implants, home station, jump clones and the next clone jump time.
- **Logbook → Losses** — the ships you lost (ship, system, time, attackers, final-blow corporation, a zKillboard link), filterable beside your entries, with **Add to journal** to start an entry titled “Lost a <ship> in <system>” in the Lesson learned category.
- **Wing → Goals** and **Logbook** — shared goals and a journal kept in this browser, with optional sync through the wing backend (turn it on per browser in the Logbook's Backup card), export, import and draft recovery. A goal can carry an ISK target, the result so far (shown as progress, with “target reached”), a checklist of steps you tick off on the goal, and notes.

Visitors without a session see public profiles plus each pilot's shared skill total and queue. Everything else needs that pilot's own session.

A browser stays connected: its session is kept in `localStorage` and renews itself on every daily visit, expiring only after 30 days without use. Redeploys don't log anyone out, because sessions, encrypted EVE authorizations and the encryption key all live in Cloudflare, and the backend keeps syncing every 10 minutes even with no browser open. **Disconnect** ends it everywhere.

## Jobs

**Earn → Jobs** lists every industry job running for the wing (your own, plus your wingmate's when they share with you): ready-to-deliver first, then the soonest to finish, with a progress bar, time left and end time, runs (copies × runs for copy jobs), where it runs and its cost in the detail drawer. It also shows each pilot's build and science slots in use and flags idle build slots. ESI can still report a finished job as active for a few minutes; the page counts it as ready once its end time passes.

## How it works

Each pilot connects once through the Worker (`/auth/start`), which runs EVE SSO with PKCE and a confidential client, stores the refresh token encrypted in D1, and syncs ESI every 10 minutes. The browser only ever holds an opaque session that lasts 30 days from its last use (it slides forward on each daily visit); it never sees EVE tokens. See [worker/README.md](worker/README.md).

### EVE application scopes

Enable all of these on the EVE application (client ID `a94f7ccbcc8b4b0d8f7fd90fec110d2b`):

- `esi-skills.read_skills.v1`, `esi-skills.read_skillqueue.v1` (required)
- `esi-wallet.read_character_wallet.v1`
- `esi-location.read_location.v1`, `esi-location.read_ship_type.v1`
- `esi-industry.read_character_jobs.v1`, `esi-industry.read_character_mining.v1`
- `esi-clones.read_clones.v1`, `esi-clones.read_implants.v1`
- `esi-assets.read_assets.v1` (also the source of the current fit)
- `esi-markets.read_character_orders.v1` (My orders and order history)
- `esi-contracts.read_character_contracts.v1` (Ledger contracts)
- `esi-killmails.read_killmails.v1` (the loss log)
- `esi-characters.read_blueprints.v1`, `esi-planets.manage_planets.v1`, `esi-characters.read_standings.v1` (stored for the blueprint library, the Planets view and broker fees with standings)
- `esi-ui.write_waypoint.v1` (Hauling: send a route to the in-game autopilot)
- `esi-location.read_online.v1` (the Android app's live view: is the pilot online)
- Corporation data for the Android app's Corp tab, all optional: `esi-corporations.read_starbases.v1`, `esi-corporations.read_structures.v1`, `esi-assets.read_corporation_assets.v1`, `esi-wallet.read_corporation_wallets.v1`, `esi-corporations.read_corporation_membership.v1`, `esi-industry.read_corporation_jobs.v1`, `esi-planets.read_customs_offices.v1`, `esi-corporations.read_divisions.v1`. ESI also needs the in-game role (Director, Station Manager or Accountant) for most of them; a section the pilot may not read shows "needs role X" instead of failing.

A pilot who connected before a scope was added sees "Permission not granted" for that data and can press **Reconnect** to grant it.

**Both pilots must reconnect once more** to grant the nine newest scopes (the online state and the eight corporation scopes above; earlier, orders, contracts, killmails, blueprints, planets and standings). Until they do everything else keeps working. The scopes must also be ticked on the EVE application at [developers.eveonline.com](https://developers.eveonline.com/), or EVE refuses the login that asks for them.

### Wingmate sharing

On the consent page each pilot has three optional boxes, all unticked. **Share with my wingmate** (level 2): skills, wallet balance, asset summary, ship, location with a 7-day trail, industry jobs and mining ledger reach the other pilot's session through `GET /api/wing`. **Also share fit, blueprints and planets** (level 3, needs the first box): the ship's current fit, blueprints (with where they are) and planetary colonies (with their solar systems). A pilot who agreed at level 2 keeps sharing only what they agreed to and reconnects to add level 3. **Post my alerts to Discord**: independent of the other two, see below. Orders, contracts, standings and losses are read for the pilot's own screens and alerts and are never sent to the wingmate. None of it is public.

### Discord alerts

Optional. With a `DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL` secret the Worker posts one short message per new event to a Discord channel: a skill queue that is empty or ends within 24 hours, an industry job that is ready, a market order that was outbid, filled or expired, a contract that was accepted, completed or expires within 24 hours, and a new loss. Each event is sent once. Alerts go out only for a pilot who ticked **Post my alerts** on the consent page (`pilots.share_alerts`, off by default; a pilot who connected before it existed reconnects to opt in), because everyone in the channel sees them. Messages name the pilot and the item but never a system, station or region. Without the secret nothing is sent. Setup is in [worker/README.md](worker/README.md#discord-alerts).

### Market pipeline

`.github/workflows/market.yml` is scheduled every hour (and runs on demand), but GitHub drops many scheduled runs: in practice it runs a few times a day. `scripts/market-pipeline.mjs` resolves `market/watchlist.json`, adds the materials of each item's blueprint (blueprints from CCP's official static data export), screens up to 400 cheap T1 blueprints and surveys high-sec regions for NPC sell orders (365-day orders) so the Industry tab only suggests blueprints you can actually buy, with their real seller stations. ESI rate-limits market orders, so the survey is incremental: each run spends up to 20 minutes refreshing the regions not checked in the last 20 hours, the pilots' and trade hubs' regions first, and keeps its progress in the Worker. It then builds the gate map (with asteroid belts, 3D positions, CCP's schematic layout, constellations and region names), every NPC station with its services, the NPC factory/lab list and belt-ore reprocessing data from the SDE, and scans ESI orders in the five trade hubs plus every region a connected pilot is in. It keeps the best prices per NPC station and uploads the result to the Worker (`POST /api/market/ingest`, signed with an HMAC of the EVE client secret). Edit the watchlist to scan other items.

Kills and jumps need an hourly sample, which the pipeline can't promise, so the **Worker's cron** takes them: once ESI has a new hour (its `Expires`, at most hourly) a cron run reads `/universe/system_kills/` and `/universe/system_jumps/` with `If-None-Match` and keeps last hour's counts plus decayed histories of ship kills (24 h and 7 days) and jumps (24 h) in D1 (`market_docs`, migration 0007, which moved the pipeline's last history there so none was lost).

The browser reads `GET /api/market?v=2` (about 0.77 MB, revalidated with an ETag, so an unchanged market is a 304) and the static universe document `GET /api/market/universe?v=<version>` (gate map, stations, ores, combat types and skill IDs, about 1 MB), which it keeps in IndexedDB and downloads again only when the version changes. Before the split every load was 1.77 MB. Older pages that call `GET /api/market` still get everything inline. The browser adds jump distances from the pilot's position.

ESI calls use unversioned routes pinned to a compatibility date: the Worker and the pipeline send `X-Compatibility-Date: 2025-12-16`; the browser sends `compatibility_date=2025-09-26` and a generic `user_agent` as query parameters, as ESI documents for apps that can't set headers (a custom header would add a CORS preflight to every call). Move a date forward only after reading ESI's changelog (`/meta/changelog`).

Not done: the Worker could start the heavy pipeline itself with a `workflow_dispatch` call (`POST /repos/<owner>/<repo>/actions/workflows/market.yml/dispatches`), which GitHub doesn't drop. That needs a GitHub token with Actions write access stored as a Worker secret, so it is left as an option.

## Develop

```sh
npm ci
npm run check
npm test              # unit + Worker tests
npm run test:e2e      # browser tests against a mocked backend
npm start             # http://localhost:8000/
```

Pushing to `main` runs the tests, deploys the Worker and Pages, then runs live smoke tests.

The page is static ES modules with no build step. `app.js` boots the app and routes between views; `ui/` holds the shell and shared helpers, and each destination owns `ui/screens/<name>.js` (its markup, renderers and listeners) and `styles/<name>.css`. Design tokens live in `styles/tokens.css`. The pure planning modules (`model.js`, `market.js`, `industry.js`, `route.js`, …) stay at the root with their unit tests. Earn and Navigate (the planners, the Star map and its 3D drawing) load on the first visit to one of their views or when the page is idle; their markup is split into `ui/screens/*-markup.js` so the page can build every screen at boot, and `index.html` preloads the boot graph (`tests/package.test.js` keeps the two in step). The Haul plan's route searches run in a module Worker (`ui/haulworker.js`, driven by `ui/haulplan.js`) and fall back to the page if a Worker can't start; the same Worker runs the Sell planner (`sell.js`: where to sell what the ships and hangars hold, and each ship's route), which loads only when it is first used. `tests/planners.test.js` checks the optimised planners against reference versions and carries time budgets on a 5,400-system payload.

## Security and observability

- **Pipeline signing.** Market uploads are signed with a `PIPELINE_SECRET` GitHub secret (create it once; see `worker/README.md`), so a captured request can't be replayed. Without it the old fixed token still works, with a warning.
- **Delete my data.** Beside Disconnect, **Delete my data** removes everything the backend stores about a pilot (journal entries and settings, EVE authorization, sessions) after two confirmations. Entries saved in your browser stay there. Wing goals belong to both pilots and stay.
- **Rate limits, body caps and a framing guard.** Cloudflare rate limits protect login start, the public market and the journal. The page won't render inside another site's frame. The published page's Content-Security-Policy allows only this deployment's Worker; the source keeps `*.workers.dev` so local and browser tests can run, and `scripts/configure-frontend.mjs` pins it at deploy time.
- **Health.** `GET /health/freshness` publishes ages only. `.github/workflows/health.yml` checks it hourly, opens one issue labelled `health` while data is stale and closes it when it recovers. Workers Logs hold one JSON line per request and cron run, with no tokens, ids or locations.
- **Supply chain.** Every workflow action is pinned to a commit SHA, and the jobs that use the `github-pages` environment's secrets run only from `main` (also set a branch rule on the environment in Settings → Environments).
- EVE Online's trademark notice (CCP licence §7.1) is in the page footer.

## Staying signed in and keeping your data

- **Sessions are per device and per browser.** Connecting a pilot stores an opaque dashboard session in this browser's `localStorage`; it lasts 30 days from its last use, and every tab of that browser shares it. Another phone, another browser, a private window or a Home Screen copy of the app (it has its own storage, separate from Safari's) needs its own **Connect**.
- **Safari clears it after a week away.** Safari deletes everything a site's scripts stored (sessions, the journal, drafts) after 7 days of Safari use without visiting the site. Web apps added to the Home Screen are exempt ([WebKit tracking prevention](https://webkit.org/tracking-prevention/)), so on an iPhone or iPad open Echelon in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, and use it from there. iOS Safari shows a one-time tip about this (`ui/installhint.js`). The app ships a web app manifest and icons (`manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/`, PNGs rendered from `icons/icon.svg` by `scripts/render-icons.mjs`) and no service worker, so nothing is cached past a deploy.
- **The journal can live on the backend too.** Turn on **Sync goals and entries with the wing backend** in the Logbook's Backup card and goals and entries come back on any device once a pilot connects (see below).
- **Export a backup file** from the same card for an offline copy; **Restore or merge a backup** brings it back on any device.

GitHub Pages also serves every `*.github.io` project of this account from one origin, so they all share its `localStorage`. `worker/README.md` sketches moving to a custom domain, which would fix that and let the session move into an HttpOnly cookie.

## Notes storage

Goals and entries live in `localStorage` (`eve-flight-journal:v1`); unsaved Logbook and goal drafts live in `sessionStorage`. Unreadable saved data blocks writes and offers a raw recovery download. A change made in another tab blocks stale saves. After the first save the app asks the browser to keep its site data, and the Backup card says when this browser last exported.

Each goal and entry carries `updatedAt` (ms since 1970), set on every save, edit and tick. Restoring a backup offers **Merge** (the default): each record keeps whichever copy was edited last, records from before edit times count as oldest, a tie keeps this browser's copy, and nothing is deleted. **Replace everything** asks again, then makes the journal match the backup exactly, deletes included (on synced devices too). Fields a newer build adds to a record are kept by older tabs, devices, backups and the Worker: they must be plain JSON without prototype keys, at most 8,000 characters per record (`EXTRA_MAX` in `model.js`). Backups stay version 1, and older ones still import.

Browsers can still clear site data (Safari does after 7 days without a visit), so the Backup card offers **Sync goals and entries with the wing backend**. With it on, the browser keeps working from its own copy and syncs on load, when the tab returns, every 5 minutes and shortly after each change (`ui/journalsync.js`, rules in `ui/syncplan.js`). Goals belong to the wing; an entry belongs to the pilot it's written for when that pilot is connected here, else to the pilot flying here, and only that pilot's sessions can read it. Records are encrypted in D1 and survive **Disconnect**. If a record was changed on two devices before they synced, the backend's version keeps its place and the other is kept as a “(conflict copy)”; a local edit beats a remote delete. Tabs with sync on adopt each other's saves instead of blocking.

The same switch syncs two settings of the pilot flying here: systems marked scanned in Explore (`eve-explored:v1`, marks older than 90 days are dropped, at most 1000 kept) and the Haul plan (`eve-haul:v1`, `ui/prefs.js`). They're stored as one `pref` record per pilot per setting (`explored:<characterId>`, `haul:<characterId>`) that only that pilot can read or write. They merge instead of making copies: scanned marks combine, keeping the newest scan per system, and the haul plan a person changed last wins. They aren't part of a backup file.
